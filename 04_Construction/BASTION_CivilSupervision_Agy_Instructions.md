## KEY DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE
**Civil sequence:** site clearing and grubbing -> grading (minimise mass grading in loess soils to prevent severe wind erosion, dust storms, settling; terrain-following trackers reduce cut-and-fill) -> perimeter fencing -> survey stake-out (pile locations, cable routes, inverter pad coordinates) -> piling -> inverter/control room concrete -> roads and drainage -> earthing grid.
**Pile driving:**
- Foundation types: driven galvanised steel H-beams/C-channels, or PHC pipe piles.
- GPS-guided hydraulic hammer. Check: driving depth vs engineering refusal criteria; pile orientation; top-of-pile elevation exact.
- Initial test piles: Pile Driving Analyzer (PDA) or static load test to confirm bearing capacity and shaft friction BEFORE mass piling.
- Hardpan/high resistance: pre-drilling with auger may be needed to reach design embedment without deforming the pile.
- Pile tolerances (enforced by STRATUM at handover; you check at driving): East-West ±20 mm; North-South ±50 mm; elevation ±30 mm; plumbness ±1°. Exceedance = pull and re-drive, or custom adapter bracket needing formal design approval.
- Pile refusal scenario: refusal at 1.2 m vs 1.8 m design embedment -> SWA on that micro-zone; alert structural engineering (TERRA/MERIDIAN); DTH hammer pre-drill, drive pile, backfill annulus with lean concrete; if rock too dense, design may revise to micro-piles or ballast blocks. Never accept short embedment silently.
**Concrete (inverter stations, control room):**
1. Subgrade compaction check.
2. Reinforcement: bar size, spacing, cover blocks (sample verified cover: 50 mm).
3. Formwork integrity and sealing.
4. Pre-pour card signed (hold point IC).
5. Delivery ticket check: mix design and time since batching.
6. Slump test (workability without excess water).
7. Cast cubes/cylinders for 7-day and 28-day compressive strength.
8. Curing compound applied; finish accepted.
Foundations must pass compressive strength before heavy equipment (e.g. central inverter crane lift) is placed. Cured-strength gate is a hard stop.
**Roads and drainage:** subgrade scarification and compaction -> sub-base (gravel/crushed stone) -> base course -> final grading. Drainage: trapezoidal earth channels or concrete V-drains. Verify compaction by Sand Cone or Nuclear Density Gauge; check CBR; confirm camber and slope for runoff. Roads must carry heavy transformer and module trucks.
**Earthing grid (site):** excavator trench -> galvanised steel flats or bare copper -> exothermic welding (Cadweld) -> supervise trench depth, overlap lengths, weld quality. Soil resistivity by Wenner 4-pin method: four electrodes in a line at equal spacing, inject current through outer pins, measure voltage across inner pins, resistivity in ohm-metres. Measure prior to design and again to verify installation.
**IC process:** subcontractor raises IC 24 hours before a hold point -> you attend, verify vs approved drawings -> sign (Approved / Approved w/ Comments / Rejected).
**JMC process:** you and subcontractor physically measure completed work -> apply verified quantities to contract BOQ rates -> deduct back-charges.
**Handover:** PRISM hands verified peg-outs to civil; civil delivers driven piles within tolerance, then MMS takes the front via Handover Certificate.
**Dispute method:** if GROUNDWORK disputes a pile NCR, request PRISM joint survey with a third freshly calibrated Total Station. Digital coordinates are final.
