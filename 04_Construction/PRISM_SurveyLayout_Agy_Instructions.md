## KEY DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE
**Layout process:** establish primary geodetic baseline with Real-Time Kinematic (RTK) GPS bases -> from the control points, the subcontractor's surveyors peg out exact centre points of tracker drive pillars and boundary markers.
**Audit role:**
- Randomly sample 10% to 15% of the subcontractor's pegged points using a highly calibrated Total Station BEFORE giving clearance for pile driving.
- Tolerance: if sampled points deviate beyond engineering tolerances (e.g. ±20 mm horizontal for tracker pillars) the ENTIRE block is rejected and the subcontractor re-surveys at its own cost.
- Related pile tolerances (for as-built and NCR checks): E-W ±20 mm; N-S ±50 mm; elevation ±30 mm; plumbness ±1°.
**As-built survey:** continuous as sections complete. RTK rover captures X, Y, Z of every installed pile, underground cable route and concrete foundation. Living document; critical for O&M troubleshooting; mandatory deliverable for Mechanical Completion. MV cable: GPS coordinates, depth and phase arrangement recorded before backfill; route markers at 50 m intervals, direction changes and every joint.
**Pile record link:** use the Pile Installation Record columns (Pile ID, Block, Northing Y, Easting X, Target Elev Z, Actual Elev Z, deviations, plumbness, Pass/Fail). Sample failure: B4-R12-P2, Z deviation +35 mm vs ±30 mm tolerance -> FAIL, NCR.
**Dispute resolution (pile NCR challenged):** joint survey by PRISM and subcontractor surveyor using a third, freshly calibrated Total Station. Digital coordinates are final. If subcontractor still refuses to rectify, EPC hires a third party to pull/re-drive piles and deducts the cost from the next JMC.
**Instrument discipline:** calibrated Total Station only; log calibration date and ID for every audit. A sagging tape measure is not survey evidence.
