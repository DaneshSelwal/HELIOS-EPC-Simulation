## KEY DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE

### Environmental aspects and impacts (solar construction, arid Uzbekistan)
- Land disturbance and erosion: vegetation clearing destroys root systems. Loess soil, high erosion risk. Topsoil loss and dust in wind events.
- Dust: PM2.5 and PM10. Also a performance risk, as soiling of modules can derate output (up to ~30% per build brief; indicative).
- Noise and vibration: pile driving, generators, heavy plant. Impacts fauna and nearby residents.
- Water: dust suppression and concrete batching strain scarce aquifers/municipal supply.
- Waste: packaging (pallets, plastic), damaged PV modules (heavy metals), camp domestic waste, oily rags, filters.
- Spills: transformer oil, diesel, hydraulic fluid.
- Biodiversity: habitat loss from fencing, grading, human presence.
Mitigation hierarchy: Avoid, Minimize, Restore, Offset.

### ESMP structure
Baseline environmental conditions -> impact assessment -> mitigation measures (sub-plans: Dust, Waste, Spills, Water, Biodiversity) -> monitoring plan/matrix (what, limit, frequency, responsibility) -> emergency response (spills) -> waste management plan -> grievance mechanism. Legal base: Uzbek environmental law, SanPiN standards, IFC PS1.

### Monitoring programme
- Dust: stationary sensors at site boundary, PM2.5 and PM10 continuous. SanPiN 0293-11 MPC for PM10 = 500 ug/m3 (as stated in knowledge base; confidence MEDIUM that this is the governing limit; lenders may apply a stricter IFC/WHO-based value in the ESMP, check the ESMP matrix before reporting). Exceedance -> increase suppression immediately, notify AEGIS, log.
- Noise: calibrated hand-held decibel meters at perimeter and nearest sensitive receptor. KMK 2.01.08-96: 55 dBA day / 45 dBA night in residential areas (maximum continuous). Exceedance -> adjust piling hours, enclosures, notify AEGIS.
- Water quality: if groundwater dewatering is running, monitor discharge and receiving water per ESMP. No uncontrolled discharge.
- Spills and waste: daily visual check using inspection checklists; waste tracked by category.

### Dust suppression management
Dedicated water bowser fleet continuously spraying haul roads and active earthworks. Speed limit 20 km/h. Windbreak fencing. Chemical palliatives on long-term static dirt roads. Halt bulk earthworks above safe wind limits (no figure in the knowledge base; interim default 10 m/s, same as module handling limit, confirm with AEGIS and set in ESMP; confidence LOW-MEDIUM). Module installation: no-work above 10 m/s. Watch water use: track m3 for the monthly report.

### Waste management
Segregate in multi-bin systems: Hazardous (oil filters, contaminated rags, paint cans, damaged solar panels), Recyclable (pallets, cardboard, metals, clean plastics), General domestic. Hazardous waste needs a chain-of-custody manifest and may only go via licensed facilities approved by the Uzbekistan Ministry of Ecology, Environmental Protection and Climate Change. No burning, no burying, no unlicensed haulers.

### Spill response (preventive + reactive)
Prevention: secondary containment bunds holding 110% of the largest vessel, drip trays under all static plant (diesel generators), spill kits at visible locations. Response:
1. Stop the source (plug leak, upright drum, emergency pump shutoff).
2. Contain with booms and absorbent granules/sand. NO drainage to ground, drains, or watercourses.
3. Clean up: contaminated soil and absorbents into hazardous waste bags, logged on the manifest.
4. Report volume and GPS location to you for logging and remediation verification.
Transformer oil spill: licensed waste contractor for oil disposal, incident report within 24 hours, notify client and lender (route through AEGIS; major spills fall in the IFC 48h formal window). Ref scenario: 220kV transformer fill, hose flange rupture -> pump shutoff, boom, excavate soil, manifest.

### Biodiversity
Ecological sweep surveys before civil works (reptiles, nesting birds). Relocate critical species per ESMP. No hunting, poaching, feeding wildlife. High-visibility fenced No-Go ecological buffers around sensitive habitat and watercourses. Report any machinery encroachment.

### Grievance mechanism (IFC PS2 workers, PS4 communities)
- Publicly posted at site gate and community notice boards, multi-lingual. Channels: locked drop boxes at gates, hotline, WhatsApp number, community liaison offices. Anonymous submissions accepted. Confidentiality guaranteed. No retaliation.
- Register fields: date / complainant / issue / response / close-out (plus ref, category, owner).
- SLA: acknowledge within 7 days. Investigate and communicate proposed resolution within 14 to 30 days depending on complexity (report % resolved within 14 days). Appeals process available.
- Community Liaison Officers bridge to the local Makhalla (traditional community councils): communicate convoys and noisy works in advance, manage local hiring expectations, address dust and road damage grievances before they escalate to protests or blockades.

### IFC PS2 labour monitoring
- Hours: build brief ceiling = 48 hrs/week + up to 12 overtime. Uzbek Labour Code standard week = 40 hrs (knowledge base). Apply the stricter reading: flag anyone above 40 as overtime, and escalate above 48 standard-equivalent or any week beyond 60 total. Mandatory weekly rest day. Confirm statutory overtime cap with PERMIT/Legal (confidence MEDIUM).
- Wages: at or above minimum, paid in full, on time, directly to worker, readable payslips.
- No workers under 18 on hazardous construction work. Verify age documents.
- No forced or bonded labour. Red flag: any subcontractor holding worker passports.
- Freedom of association and non-discrimination.
- Subcontractor audits are required by lenders. PS2 and PS4 (community, unarmed security unless documented threat) feed the monthly report.
