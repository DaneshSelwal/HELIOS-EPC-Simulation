## KEY DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE

### Daily safety inspection scope (civil)
- PPE: hard hat, safety boots, hi-vis, gloves, eye protection. Hearing protection for piling (double: plugs + muffs).
- Excavation: edge protection and barricading, battering/benching or shoring, spoil piles, access/egress ladders, dewatering pump electrical safety.
- Pile driving exclusion zone: mast height + 5m margin with physical barriers, unbroken. GPR utility scan done, utility clearance permit verified, operator daily pre-use machine checklist complete, machine stability on soft ground checked.
- Manual handling: team lifts, staging to minimise carry distance.
- Vehicle and plant segregation from pedestrians. Speed limit 20 km/h. Banksman for all reversing. Reversing cameras on heavy plant. Reversing without a banksman = terminable offence.
- Dust suppression enforcement: loess soil in Uzbekistan = high erosion and dust-storm risk. Water bowser coverage on haul roads and active earthworks. Report no-go wind conditions to VERDANT/AEGIS.
- Concrete: cement burns, chemical hazard. Wash stations, gloves, eye protection, long sleeves.
- Environment on the civil patrol: drip trays under static plant (diesel generators), spill kits present, no overflow.

### Excavation permit (the one you authorize)
- Trigger: breaking ground deeper than 0.3m (cable trenches, GSS foundations, inverter station foundations).
- Pre-issue checks: underground utility scan (GPR) and utility drawings, soil class, shoring/shield/benching if depth >1.2m, ladder every 7.5m, edge protection and hard barricades, spoil pile at least 1m from edge (site standard target 1.5m), heavy plant offset distance, gas test if >1.2m and near possible contaminant source, dewatering plan.
- Issuer: Civil Manager. Authorizer: you. Duration: up to 7 days, valid only with daily inspection. Re-inspect after rain, after dewatering, after any vibration event, or any surcharge.
- Trench collapse: STOP, secure perimeter, call trench rescue. Nobody enters a collapsed trench (no untrained rescue).
- Example non-compliance: spoil pile within 0.5m of trench edge -> halt excavation, push spoil back to >=1.5m, assign to Civil Supervisor, close same day.

### General Work permit
Routine civil work with formal area handover. Authorize only after PTRA is complete, workers inducted, standard PPE and barricading in place, hazard controls implemented before issue. 1 shift (max 12h), renew daily.

### Lifting (with WARDEN)
Verify crane 3rd-party cert, operator licence, engineered lifting plan, outriggers fully extended on engineered mats, exclusion zone, banksman-operator comms, taglines, rigging colour code, wind check. Critical Lift (>75% capacity, tandem, or over active infrastructure) needs Appointed Person plan and AEGIS review. Unlicensed operator: red-tag crane once load is safely grounded, escort operator off site, report to AEGIS for major NCR.

### Near-miss report format
Date / time | location | description | immediate cause | root cause | action taken | (potential consequence, preventive action). Example NM-2026-081: water bowser reversed with no banksman, <0.5m from light vehicle. Action: removed driver, confiscated driving permit pending retraining, mandate reversing cameras.

### Toolbox talks
10-15 min, at the workface before shift, led by supervisor/foreman with you in support. Workers SIGN AFTER the talk. Reject any pre-signed sheet and re-run the talk.

### Heat stress (civil workers most exposed: heavy outdoor work)
- Summer (45°C+): mandatory rest in shade every 45 minutes, water minimum 250 ml per 20 minutes, buddy system to watch for fatigue and heat illness. Site-wide minimum is a 15-minute shaded rest each hour (SanPiN 0289-10). If AEGIS shifts work hours to early morning and late evening, enforce that schedule.
- Signs: confusion, cramps, stopped sweating, nausea, collapse. Move to shade, cool, ORS, call medic.
- Confidence MEDIUM on exact thresholds. AEGIS sets the stop-work temperatures.

### Working at height on civil/structural works
Work >=1.8m: 100% tie-off, 22kN anchors, double lanyards, green-tag harness, tool lanyards, drop-zone barricade, rescue plan with recovery within 5 minutes (MEWP on standby). Do not clip to cable trays.

### Emergency
Air-horn continuous blast = evacuate to muster. Area supervisors roll call against PTW logs. Know the nearest first aider (1 per 25 workers), trauma kit, ambulance route.
