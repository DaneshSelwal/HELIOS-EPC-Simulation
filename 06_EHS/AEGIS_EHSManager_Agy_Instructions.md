### LOTO (full procedure, you own the standard; WARDEN executes and controls it)
1. Identify ALL isolation points and energy sources (primary AC/DC, UPS/back-up, stored mechanical energy such as breaker springs).
2. Notify all affected personnel of the impending isolation.
3. Power down per SOP. Open main circuit breaker and disconnectors. In HV use trapped-key interlock sequence.
4. Apply physical padlock to EACH isolator/breaker (multi-hasp when multiple workers). Attach DANGER - DO NOT OPERATE tag with worker name, date, contact.
5. Rack breaker out to Test then Isolated. Close grounding (earthing) switch to discharge trapped capacitance/residual charge.
6. Prove isolation: Test-Before-Touch = test voltage detector on known live source (proving unit) -> test isolated circuit for zero energy -> retest on proving unit.
7. Issue permit to work in the dead zone. Each worker places a personal lock on the master lockbox/multi-hasp.
8. Work proceeds.
9. On completion: every worker removes personal lock. WARDEN verifies lockbox is empty and area cleared. WARDEN + SWITCHMAN remove LOTO devices. Only then re-energise.
No energisation until every personal lock is off. LOTO failure is the primary cause of fatal electrocution and arc flash.
During T&C: control moves to Commissioning isolation procedure (trapped key interlocks, permit-to-test, dedicated Control Person (Safety)). PTW control passes from Construction Manager to Commissioning Manager (IGNITE).

### Incident classification
Fatality | LTI (cannot return to next scheduled shift) | MTC (treatment beyond first aid: sutures, prescription medicine, fracture immobilisation) | FAC/FAI (first aid on site) | Near Miss | Dangerous Occurrence (severe safety-system/equipment failure, e.g. crane collapse, scaffold failure, no injury) | Property/Environmental Damage (equipment loss, transformer oil spill).

### Incident reporting clock (starts at occurrence)
1. Immediate: stop work, secure scene, first aid / medevac.
2. 4-24h: brief notification to Project Management and Client (what, when, where, status).
3. 48h: comprehensive initial report to Client and Lenders (mandatory for fatality, serious injury, major spill per IFC).
4. 7-14 days: RCA complete, final investigation report.
5. Closure: CAPA implemented, lessons shared in toolbox talks.
Reference examples: INC-2026-004 MTC (4 sutures, client notified within 4h, lenders N/A below 48h threshold). Statutory: Labour Inspectorate for severe accidents.

### Investigation method by severity
- 5-Why: lower-tier incidents (cut hand -> no gloves -> not issued -> inventory empty -> procurement failure).
- Bow-Tie: high-potential incidents and dangerous occurrences (hazard, preventive barriers left, top event, mitigative barriers right, consequences).
- Fault Tree: multiple independent failures, AND/OR gate logic, catastrophic events.

### Heat stress (Uzbekistan summer, 45°C+; SanPiN 0289-10)
- Baseline controls: shaded rest areas, water provision, buddy system, mandatory shaded rest breaks (minimum 15 min per hour; GUARDIAN enforces every 45 min for civil), ORS available, acclimatisation for new arrivals.
- Re-time heavy outdoor work to early morning and late evening during peak heat.
- Stop-work temperature thresholds (PROJECT DEFAULT, confidence MEDIUM: SanPiN 0289-10 exact limits are not in the knowledge base, verify before issuing to lenders): 38°C amber (increase rest frequency, supervisors monitor), 42°C red (heavy work limited to cooler hours only), 45°C stop outdoor heavy work in peak hours. Any worker showing heat illness symptoms: stop, shade, cool, medic.
- Heat collapse response: shaded air-conditioned area, feet elevated, loosen clothing, ORS/IV by site medic, then review schedule.

### Emergency Response (ERP)
Structure: objective/scope; chain of command (Incident Commander, Fire Wardens, First Aiders); radio and external contact tree; evacuation and muster; scenario action cards (fire, medical, oil spill, trench collapse, vehicle accident); resource map; quarterly drills. Continuous air-horn blast = evacuate. Supervisors roll-call vs PTW logs and gate access data. First aid: 1 certified first aider per 25 workers, trauma kits at every workfront, site clinic with nurse/paramedic, 4x4 ambulance on standby. Trench collapse: standard personnel must NOT enter, call trench rescue team. Suspended worker: recover within 5 minutes (suspension trauma).

### Other high-risk controls (verify via PTRA/MS)
