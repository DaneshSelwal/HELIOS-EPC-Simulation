### Section 7: Top Issues / Blockers & Critical Path Notes
1. **EVT-001 — Geotechnical Hard Rock Strata in Block 6 (Pilot DTH Pre-Drilling Operational)**:
   - **Issue Summary**: Rock stratum encountered at 0.8m depth across Block 6 preventing conventional hydraulic pile ramming to required design embedment (>1.5m).
   - **Current Status**: Down-The-Hole (DTH) hammer drill rig and high-pressure compressor fully operational under active PTW. Completed 35 pilot pre-drilled holes (180–220mm diameter + 500mm rock socket) on Day 006, confirming feasibility and drilling rate.
   - **Mitigation & Lookahead**: Pre-drilling run-rate expected to ramp up as crew stabilizes cycle times. Hydraulic piling in standard strata (Blocks 1–5) maintained strong pace with 920 ramming piles installed today.
   - **Commercial & Contractual**: Delay event tracked under FIDIC Yellow Book Sub-Clause 4.12 (Unforeseeable Physical Conditions). Machine hours, auxiliary fuel, drill bit consumption, and mobilization costs tracked under cost code `CC-VAR-EVT-001`.
2. **EVT-002 — UzbekEnergo Revised Grid Code / Primary Frequency Response (Transmittal TRN-EPC-CLI-0012 Submitted)**:
   - **Issue Summary**: UzbekEnergo grid code directive requiring Primary Frequency Response (PFR) capability with droop response <2 seconds.
   - **Current Action**: Technical response package finalized by ARCHON and VOLTA. Formal software and design transmittal **TRN-EPC-CLI-0012** submitted to UzbekEnergo, demonstrating compliance via PPC firmware modification without requiring hardware alterations to 220kV instrument transformers.
   - **Schedule & Contractual Impact**: EOT notice maintained under FIDIC Sub-Clause 20.1 to safeguard project baseline float. GSS critical path activity DEL-002 / FRAG-EVT-002 closely monitored pending formal client acceptance of TRN-EPC-CLI-0012.
3. **Data Governance & MIS Controls**:
   - Piling progress reconciled: 920 ramming piles certified by Civil QC (PLUMBLINE) and Civil Lead (BASTION). Cumulative total stands at 10,970 piles (71.23% of 15,400 scope).
   - DTH pilot pre-drilling verified separately at 35 holes; uncertified claims rejected per MIS credit rules.
