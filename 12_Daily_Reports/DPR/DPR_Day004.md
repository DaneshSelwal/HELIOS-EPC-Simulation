### Section 7: Top Issues / Blockers & Critical Path Notes
1. **EVT-001 — Geotechnical Hard Rock Strata in Block 6 (HOLD & Mitigation)**:
   - **Issue Summary**: Encountered rock stratum at 0.8m depth across Block 6 (design assumed soil refusal >1.5m). Standard ramming rigs cannot achieve embedment without refusal.
   - **Current Action**: Block 6 piling remains on **HOLD**. Piling activities re-sequenced to maximize production in Blocks 1–5, achieving full daily target (900 piles/day) with 4 hydraulic rigs.
   - **Mitigation Status**: Approved Field Design Change FDC-CIV-001 mandates pre-drilling. Mobilization of Down-The-Hole (DTH) hammer drilling rig is actively underway by GROUNDWORK. Rig arrival and trial pre-drilling anticipated shortly.
   - **Contractual & Commercial**: COUNSEL has logged delay event under FIDIC Yellow Book Sub-Clause 4.12 (Unforeseeable Physical Conditions). Cost impacts tracked under variation cost code `CC-VAR-EVT-001`.
2. **EVT-002 — UzbekEnergo Revised Grid Code / Primary Frequency Response (PFR)**:
   - **Issue Summary**: UzbekEnergo issued updated grid code compliance directive mandating Primary Frequency Response (PFR) capability across the solar plant and substation interface.
   - **Current Action**: Technical review of PFR requirements is actively underway by ARCHON, VOLTA, and CIPHER. Evaluation covers inverter active power curtailment / droop control algorithms, Power Plant Controller (PPC) response time (<2 sec), and substation protection philosophy resubmission.
   - **Schedule & Contractual Impact**: EOT notice logged under FIDIC Sub-Clause 20.1 to protect project schedule baseline. KRONOS modeling impact on engineering freeze and grid compliance milestones.
3. **Data Governance & MIS Controls**:
