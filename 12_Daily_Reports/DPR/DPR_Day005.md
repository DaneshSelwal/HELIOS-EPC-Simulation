### Section 7: Top Issues / Blockers & Critical Path Notes
1. **EVT-001 — Geotechnical Hard Rock Strata in Block 6 (DTH Rig On Site & Pre-Drilling Induction)**:
   - **Issue Summary**: Encountered rock stratum at 0.8m depth across Block 6 (design assumed soil refusal >1.5m). Standard ramming rigs cannot achieve embedment without refusal.
   - **Current Status**: Down-The-Hole (DTH) hammer drilling rig and 21-bar high-pressure compressor convoy arrived on site at NW gate. Site induction, spark arrestor check, and gate safety verification in progress by AEGIS and BASTION. PTW issuance proceeding.
   - **Mitigation & Lookahead**: Pilot pre-drilling (180–220mm dia + 500mm rock socket) commencing Day 6 under approved Field Design Change FDC-CIV-001. Piling production in Blocks 1–5 maintains accelerated run-rate (950 piles/day) with 4 hydraulic rigs.
   - **Contractual & Commercial**: COUNSEL has logged delay event under FIDIC Yellow Book Sub-Clause 4.12 (Unforeseeable Physical Conditions). Plant hours, rig wear, and auxiliary costs tracked under variation cost code `CC-VAR-EVT-001`.
2. **EVT-002 — UzbekEnergo Revised Grid Code / Primary Frequency Response (PFR Review Concluding)**:
   - **Issue Summary**: UzbekEnergo issued updated grid code compliance directive mandating Primary Frequency Response (PFR) capability across the solar plant and substation interface.
   - **Current Action**: Technical review of PFR requirements is actively concluding today by ARCHON, VOLTA, and CIPHER. Evaluation confirms PPC firmware droop-curve algorithms (<2 sec response) vs potential hardware impacts on 220kV CT/PT instrument transformers.
   - **Schedule & Contractual Impact**: EOT notice logged under FIDIC Sub-Clause 20.1 to protect project schedule baseline. GSS critical path activity DEL-002 / FRAG-EVT-002 actively tracked with zero float buffer.
