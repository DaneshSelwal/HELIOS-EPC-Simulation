### Vehicle fleet

### Visa and work permit (foreign workers, Uzbekistan)
Sequence: labour market test (prove skill not available locally) -> corporate quota (Employer Permit) -> individual Work Permit (Confirmation) -> Type E employment visa at Uzbek embassy -> arrival -> register at migration office within 3 days -> obtain PINFL.
Authority: Agency for External Labor Migration. Quota 30-60 days; individual confirmation 15-30 days. Plan 4-6 weeks end to end per worker minimum and start earlier for critical roles.
Highly Qualified Specialists (over USD 60,000/yr plus qualifying degree): 3-year permit, statutory fee 440,000 UZS.
Business visa vs. work visa: a business visa allows meetings, inspections and supervision only. It does NOT allow physical work. Tourist visa for work = fines, project suspension, deportation. If a permit is delayed: business visa for supervision only, execution by locally permitted staff, and ask PERMIT to escalate to MIIT. Never route around this.
PERMIT owns the application; you own the dossier inputs, dates, and the local HR agency coordination.

### Accommodation standards (IFC PS2 / EBRD PR2)
- Separate sleeping areas male/female; segregated blocks for expatriates, TCNs and locals.
- Net internal area 10-12.5 m3 per person (PDF standard); also keep at least 4.5 m2 floor area per person. Apply the stricter result when the two disagree.
- No hot bedding (shift-sharing of beds). Beds at least 1.8 m x 0.6 m.
- At least 1 toilet and 1 shower per 10-15 people; anti-slip, ventilated, privacy locks.
- Clean potable water (RO), ventilation, fire exits, security, winter heating and frost-insulated pipes.
- Freedom of movement to/from accommodation must not be unduly restricted.

### Site induction process
Safety briefing + site rules + emergency procedures (muster points, evacuation) + environmental rules + grievance redress mechanism (GRM) + cultural behaviour and zero tolerance (alcohol, harassment, ecological damage) -> comprehension test -> sign induction register -> issue site ID badge and hardhat sticker -> issue PPE -> register in HR system.
Induction record columns: Employee name / Passport-ID / Company-Sub / Topics covered / Test score / Signature / Instructor / Date.
