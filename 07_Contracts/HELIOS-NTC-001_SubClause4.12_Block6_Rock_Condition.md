### 2. PARTICULARS OF THE EVENT & PHYSICAL CONDITIONS ENCOUNTERED

#### 2.1 Discovery & Date of Awareness
On **6 October 2026 (Day 2, Month 1)**, during preliminary geotechnical trial pitting and pile verification in **PV Field Block 6** (WBS `HEL.60`, Activity `HEL-CIV-PV-PLG-0600`), the Contractor encountered a competent, hard bedrock stratum at a depth of **0.80 metres below Existing Ground Level (EGL)**. 

Standard direct hydraulic pile ramming rigs encountered immediate refusal at 0.80 m. Pushing piles beyond this horizon resulted in severe structural deformation of steel profiles (flange buckling and web crippling) and immediate hammer rebound.

#### 2.2 Contractual Baseline vs. Actual Ground Conditions
- **Tender Reference Data & Employer's Requirements:** The geotechnical reference data provided by the Employer prior to the Base Date pursuant to **Sub-Clause 4.10 [Site Data]** indicated homogenous, loose-to-medium dense alluvial sand and silt formations ($N_{\text{SPT}} = 12\text{--}25$) extending continuously to depths exceeding $2.50\text{ m}$.
- **Approved Design Baseline:** In strict reliance upon the Employer's Site Data, the Issued For Construction (IFC) foundation calculation (`HEL-ST-CAL-0010`) established standard direct-driven C-channel / W-beam galvanized steel piles embedded to a design depth of **1.50 metres** to resist aerodynamic uplift ($R_t \ge 32\text{ kN}$) and tracker wind gust overturning moments per KMK 2.01.07-96.
- **Physical Impossibility & Code Non-Compliance:** Refusal at 0.80 m provides less than 35% of the design lateral passive resistance. Piles terminating at 0.80 m without positive rock anchoring fail ultimate limit state (ULS) structural criteria under Uzbek Building Code **KMK 2.02.01-98 / ShNK 2.02.01-19**, creating catastrophic overturning risks for single-axis tracker torque tubes under high wind velocity.

### 3. CONTRACTUAL QUALIFICATION UNDER SUB-CLAUSE 4.12

Pursuant to **Sub-Clause 1.1.6.8**, *"Unforeseeable"* is defined as *"not reasonably foreseeable by an experienced contractor by the date for submission of the Tender."* 

An experienced international solar EPC contractor inspecting the Site and examining the geotechnical data made available by the Employer under Sub-Clause 4.10 could not reasonably have foreseen the presence of a shallow, competent rock shelf at 0.80 m in Block 6. 

Consequently, the conditions encountered constitute **Unforeseeable Physical Conditions** under **Sub-Clause 4.12 [Unforeseeable Physical Conditions]**, entitling the Contractor to:
> *(a) an extension of time for any such delay, if completion is or will be delayed, under Sub-Clause 8.4 [Extension of Time for Completion], and*  
> *(b) payment of any such Cost, which shall be added to the Contract Price.*

### 4. STATEMENT OF CONTRACTOR'S ENTITLEMENTS

#### 4.1 Entitlement to Additional Cost (Sub-Clause 4.12 & Sub-Clause 13.3)
To overcome the rock stratum and achieve structural compliance, the Contractor has been instructed by Civil Engineering (`HEL-CV-MEM-0001`) to deploy **Option 1: Down-The-Hole (DTH) pneumatic hammer pre-drilling ($\varnothing 180\text{--}220\text{ mm}$ pilot hole + 500mm rock socket) with pressure-grouted non-shrink cementitious annulus (M15 / C12/15)**.

The Contractor hereby asserts its absolute entitlement to recover all additional Cost incurred, including but not limited to:
1. Mobilization, demobilization, and operational hire of two (2) specialized crawler-mounted DTH drill rigs and 21-bar high-pressure air compressors;
2. Rapid wear and replacement of tungsten carbide drill bits and consumable tooling in hard rock;
3. Procurement, batching, and placement of sulfate-resistant cementitious grout materials;
4. Confirmatory diamond core borehole drilling (6–8 boreholes) and laboratory testing (UCS, RQD, chemical aggressiveness) executed by MERIDIAN;
5. Installation and static load testing (axial compression ASTM D1143, tension ASTM D3689, lateral ASTM D3966) of calibrated field test piles;
6. Engineering redesign, drafting of Field Design Change `FDC-CIV-001`, and recalculation fees.

#### 4.2 Extension of Time (EOT) Reservation (Sub-Clause 8.4(b))
Per KRONOS's critical path schedule analysis (Master Delay Event Log `DEL-001` / `FRAG-EVT-001`):
- Piling cycle times in rock increase from 2.0 min/pile to 8.0–12.0 min/pile, reducing daily rig output from ~120 piles/day to 35–40 piles/day, generating an unmitigated local path delay of **+24 calendar days** in Block 6 (`HEL-CIV-PV-PLG-0600`).
- The Contractor has implemented immediate internal mitigation by re-sequencing the piling work-front: standard hydraulic ramming rigs will deploy to **Blocks 1 through 5 first** at Month 4 (`HEL.10`), shifting Block 6 to the rear work-front (Month 6–7). 
- At present data date, the Master Project Critical Path (running through the 220kV GSS autotransformer / GIS manufacturing chain) and Project COD remain protected (0 days net delay to COD; PV field array contains +45 days total float).
- **Reservation of Rights:** The Contractor expressly **reserves all rights under Sub-Clause 8.4(b)** to claim an Extension of Time for Completion should:
  - Geological delineation indicate rock extension beyond Block 6 into adjacent blocks;
  - Specialized DTH equipment mobilization face logistics or border customs delays; or
  - Any cumulative delay consume available project float.
