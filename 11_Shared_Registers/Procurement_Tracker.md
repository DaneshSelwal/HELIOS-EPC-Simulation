## 2. Procurement & Commercial Rules of Engagement

1. **Two-Envelope Bidding Protocol:**
   - **Envelope 1 (Unpriced Technical):** Complete technical compliance, data sheets, single-line diagrams, factory type tests (<5 years old), quality certificates, guarantee schedules. **Any commercial/pricing figure included in Envelope 1 results in immediate vendor disqualification.**
   - **Envelope 2 (Priced Commercial):** Commercial pricing and terms. Opened **strictly after** Technical Bid Evaluation (TBE) clearance is formally signed off by ARCHON and SCM. No price opening for technically rejected bidders.
2. **Approved Vendor List (AVL):** Bids solicited and accepted exclusively from pre-qualified suppliers on the project AVL. Unvetted suppliers are disqualified.
3. **CBE Normalization:** All commercial proposals normalized to DDP Site (HELIOS Site, Uzbekistan), incorporating inland transport, transit insurance, import duties/taxes, payment term NPV, and 25-year capitalized electrical loss evaluations (for MPT).
4. **Standard Contract Terms:**
   - Liquidated Damages (LDs): 0.5% per week of delay, capped at 10% of PO value.
   - Payment Terms: 15% Advance against Advance Payment Bank Guarantee (APBG) / 75% Irrevocable Letter of Credit (LC) at sight against shipping documents & FAT clearance / 10% Retention payable on site energization or 12 months after delivery.
   - Warranty: Minimum 24 months from commissioning or 30 months from delivery.
