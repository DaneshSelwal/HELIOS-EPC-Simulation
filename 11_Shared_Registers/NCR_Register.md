## 1. NCR Severity Classification & Governance Rules
- **Category A (`CRITICAL / STOP-WORK`)**: Threatens structural integrity, high-voltage electrical safety, or statutory compliance, or involves repeated bypassing of mandatory ITP Hold Points (`H`). Blocks Mechanical Completion (`MC`) and Provisional Acceptance (`PAC`).
- **Category B (`MAJOR`)**: Deviation from IFC drawings, IEC/KMK tolerances (e.g., pile E-W deviation $>20	ext{ mm}$, MV cable VLF breakdown, string FF $<72\%$), or approved material specs requiring formal Engineering (`ARCHON`) + Client/IE (`PATRON`/`AUDITOR`) concession (`Rework` / `Repair` / `Scrap`).
- **Category C (`MINOR`)**: Isolated workmanship defect rectifiable within 48 hours via standard repair procedure without design modification.
- **Commercial Rule**: No work covered by an open Category A or B NCR may be credited in `SIGMA` Earned Value (`BCWP`) or certified by `CHECKER`/`INVOICE` in subcontractor JMC billing.
