## KEY DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE
**Typical subcontracts (100MW):** site grading, perimeter fencing, foundation piling, MMS and module installation, trenching and cable laying, and erection/testing of the 33/220kV GSS.
**Subcontract Agreement key clauses:** scope tied to AFC drawings + firm BOQ; payment by milestone or monthly Running Account (RA) bills; LDs mirroring main contract; back-to-back provisions; pay-when-paid; defects liability period; performance bond; insurance; dispute resolution; indemnification.
**Back-to-back flow-down items:** LDs, warranty, specifications, dispute resolution, plus any client-imposed risk, penalty or technical obligation for the sub's scope. Pay-when-paid legally allows EPC to withhold until the owner pays the corresponding amount: protects EPC cash flow.
**Subcontract Agreement vs Work Order:**
- Subcontract Agreement: complex, long-term, high-risk scope (e.g. total mechanical erection); full legal framework, performance bonds, insurance, indemnification.
- Work Order: short-duration, low-risk, transactional (local security, temporary fencing, water bowser); unit rates and immediate deliverables; no heavy legal architecture; issued under a Master Service Agreement.
Work Order example: WO-HEL-CIV-005 under MSA-2025-09, Tashkent Earthworks Co., excavation/backfill of 5km 33kV trench per AFC-TR-01, $4.50/m3, joint measurement sheet signed weekly by EPC site engineer, hard barricading and daily toolbox talks mandatory.
**Evaluation criteria:** mobilisation speed, ownership of specialised equipment (hydraulic pile drivers, heavy excavators), manpower histograms, safety and environmental record in Central Asia. Bid process mirrors equipment procurement (two-envelope, technical first) but weights local execution capacity.
**Claims handling:** require formal variation notice within the contract time bar; check against AFC drawings and BOQ; unrecorded engineering addition → VO via COUNSEL; in-scope → reject in writing.
