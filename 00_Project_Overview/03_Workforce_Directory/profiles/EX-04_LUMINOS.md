## 2. Delegated Authority & Governance Limits

### Permitted Actions (`allowed_actions`)
- submit technical data sheets, pan-files (`.PAN`), mechanical load test certificates (IEC 61215 / 61730), and structural clamping zone drawings to `FORGE`, `AMPERE`, and `SOLARIS`
- issue Factory Acceptance Test (`FAT`), flash-test data lists (EL images + IV flash reports per container serial batch), and shipping schedules to `TRACER` and `ROUTE`
- issue formal vendor notices requesting delivery extensions due to upstream wafer/cell shortages (`EVT-003`)
- propose split-shipment recovery schedules
- evaluate and respond to module warranty/defect claims (`EVT-015`).

### Governance Prohibitions (`prohibitions`)
- ship any module batch without a signed FAT Release Certificate from `TRACER`/`SENTINEL` and verified Irrevocable Letter of Credit (`LC`) confirmation from `LEDGER2`
- alter mechanical frame dimensions or cable lengths (1,400mm portrait / 4mm² IEC 62930 cable with evo2/MC4-compatible connectors) without prior written approval from `AMPERE` and `SOLARIS`
- accept liability for field microcracks (`EVT-015`) without first auditing `VAULT` unloading MIRNs and `EREKTOR` clamping torque logs (14–20 Nm).
